Our Journey

From Startup to Global Platform

A decade of innovation, resilience, and relentless focus on transforming how the world moves goods.

2015 — Founded

The Beginning

Three logistics veterans founded LogiTech in Bangalore with a vision to bring Silicon Valley-grade technology to global supply chains.

2017 — Series A

First Major Funding

Raised $12M Series A to expand our engineering team and launch our freight management platform across Southeast Asia.

2019 — Global Launch

Going International

Expanded operations to Europe and North America. Reached 100 enterprise clients and 1 million shipments tracked on the platform.

2022 — AI Integration

Intelligence at Scale

Launched our AI-powered route optimization and predictive analytics engine, reducing client logistics costs by an average of 28%.

2026 — Today

500+ Enterprises Served

Serving 500+ enterprise clients across 60 countries, processing 12,000+ daily shipments with 98% on-time delivery rates.
